Shakshuka with feta

Serves 2. Ready in 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (14 oz)
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 oz feta
  • cilantro and bread to serve

Method

Soften the onion and pepper in olive oil for 8 minutes. Add garlic and spices for 1 minute, then the tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes until thick. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 6 to 8 minutes. Crumble over the feta and cilantro.
