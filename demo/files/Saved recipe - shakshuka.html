Shakshuka with feta

Serves 2. Ready in 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400 g)
  • 4 eggs
  • 75 g feta
  • coriander and bread to serve

Method

Soften the onion and pepper in olive oil for 8 minutes. Add garlic and spices for 1 minute, then the tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes until thick. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 6 to 8 minutes. Crumble over the feta and coriander.
