Questions, answered

Is the free version really everything?

Yes. Self-hosted Archive is the whole product. The paid plans are for when you would rather we ran it.

Can I move between plans?

Any time. Your archive is one folder, so moving from hosted to self-hosted, or back, is an export and an import.

Who can see my data on a hosted plan?

Each archive runs in its own isolated instance. We do not read it, and it never trains anyone's AI.
